The Mission

PROJECT 347 is building a global talent discovery and athlete development pathway focused on identifying athletic potential for Australian Football.

Instead of waiting for talent to enter traditional systems, we search for athletes with the physical profile, athletic qualities and competitive mentality to develop into footballers.

The Model
