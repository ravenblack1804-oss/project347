Find athletic potential where others may not look.

PROJECT 347 combines Australian Football development with a wider scouting philosophy — identifying athletes whose size, movement, athleticism and competitive traits may translate into new roles and opportunities.

02 / CORE SPORT

Australian Rules Football is the center of PROJECT 347.

AFL and WAFL environments form the sporting core of the project, with particular interest in identifying and developing athletic profiles suited to roles such as Ruck and Key Position Player.
AUSTRALIAN FOOTBALL

Height. Athleticism. Football potential.

PROJECT 347 is designed around the idea that elite football potential can exist outside the traditional pathway and can sometimes be found in athletes developed by entirely different sports.

03 / CROSSOVER TALENT

Different sport. Transferable advantage.

Basketball and Gaelic Football provide two clear examples of how elite attributes developed elsewhere can potentially translate into Australian Football.
Basketball → Ruck / KPP

Height meets vertical athleticism.

Very tall basketball athletes can bring reach, jumping ability, coordination and aerial instincts that may translate naturally into Ruck and Key Position development.

Gaelic Football → Running Roles

Movement meets field intelligence.

Gaelic Football can produce athletes with endurance, ball movement, space awareness and running patterns that may suit roles such as wing or half-back.

04 / A WIDER SPORTING NETWORK

Australian Football first. A broader sporting vision around it.

Rugby League, cricket, athletics and tennis represent part of the wider sporting context around PROJECT 347 — bringing different performance cultures, skills and perspectives into the larger vision.
MULTI-SPORT THINKING

One sporting vision. Many sources of excellence.

PROJECT 347 places Australian Rules Football at the center, while remaining open to talent, knowledge and athletic influence from across the wider sporting landscape.
